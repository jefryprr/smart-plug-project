# POSITION / Pick-and-Place — Provisional

**Status:** `PROVISIONAL / NOT_AN_ASSEMBLY_RELEASE`

`POSITION.csv` is exported from the routed 100 × 70 mm carrier in KiCad 10.0.8.
It lists all footprints, including through-hole connectors. Coordinates are in
mm relative to the board origin; verify rotation/side conventions against the
assembly drawing before use.

This file is **not** an assembly release. The board has no approved assembly
drawing, no physical fit check, and no component approval. Do not send it to an
assembly house until `F4 FABRICATION_RELEASED`.
