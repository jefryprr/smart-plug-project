# Wiring Harnesses — REV_D_CORE_ONLY

**Status:** `LOGICAL/RENDERED` / `PHYSICAL_NOT_TESTED`

WireViz diagrams in this folder are **cable/interface diagrams**, not a
substitute for the PCB netlist (`hardware/carrier/netlist.csv`). CORE-only carrier connectors are keyed JST-XH except the DevKit sockets. OLED/RTC/external RGB are not part of this build. The PSU board uses 5.08 mm screw terminals (TB1/TB2) and keyed JST-XH outputs (J5/J6/J7).

## Connector map

| Diagram connector | PCB reference | Function |
|---|---|---|
| `J1A` | 1×22 socket | ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1 **J1** (3V3, GPIO, 5V, GND) |
| `J1B` | 1×22 socket | ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1 **J3** (GND, GPIO, USB pins) |
| `J2` | JST-XH 4 | PZEM-004T V4 TTL: RX, TX, GND; pin 1 NC (5 V from PSU J7) |
| `J3` | JST-XH 4 | Relay module: GND, IN1, IN2; pin 1 NC (5 V from PSU J6) |
| `J6` | JST-XH 2 | Button: BUTTON, GND |
| `J8` | JST-XH 2 | 5 V input from the REV_D_CORE_ONLY PSU board J5 |

## DevKit orientation (critical)

The DevKitC-1 has **two separate 1×22 headers**, not a 2×22 IDC header.

- Row spacing: **22.86 mm**.
- J1A = DevKit J1; J1B = DevKit J3.
- Pin 1 of both sockets is at the **left** end; the DevKit USB ports are at the
  **right** end on this carrier.
- Insert the DevKit only with the USB ports facing the `USB end ->` silkscreen.
- Reverse insertion will put 5 V/3.3 V onto GPIOs. The carrier is SELV-only, but
  the DevKit can be destroyed. Add a key/blocker or mark the board before use.

## Signal notes

- PZEM TTL is 5 V. The 10k/20k divider and 74HCT125 are **on the carrier**, not
  in the cable. Do not add an external UART isolator; the PZEM internal
  optocouplers are the barrier.
- Relay/PZEM 5 V is **not** distributed by the carrier. Use the PSU J6/J7
  2-pin cables; J2/J3 pin 1 is intentionally NC.
- The relay module is active-LOW. J3 pins 3/4 are tied together on the carrier
  and driven by one AO3400; they are not independent GPIOs.
- The relay module is **not** a mechanically linked DPST.
- PE is never switched or fused.
- USB must not be connected while mains is energized.
