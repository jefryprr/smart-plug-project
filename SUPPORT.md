## I found a bug

Open a GitHub issue using the bug report template. Include:

- what you expected;
- what happened;
- exact command/version/commit;
- logs or screenshots with secrets removed;
- whether the issue is software or hardware-related.

## I have a security issue

Do **not** open a public issue. Follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

## I have a hardware safety question

This repository cannot provide personal mains safety supervision or emergency assistance. Read [`docs/SAFETY.md`](docs/SAFETY.md) and consult a qualified engineer/lab. Do not energize the prototype from mains.

## I want to contribute

Read [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md), [`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), and [`ROADMAP.md`](ROADMAP.md).

## What maintainers cannot help with

- Remote mains energization or live troubleshooting.
- Certification/regulatory sign-off.
- Guaranteeing that a third-party module (HLK, PZEM, relay, socket, fuse, MOV, TCO) is genuine or safe.
- Production deployment credentials, domains, or paid services.
- Legal advice.

## Response expectations

This is a prototype project maintained on a best-effort basis. There is no SLA. Security reports are prioritized over feature requests. Hardware safety claims require evidence and may be closed if they consist only of assertions.
