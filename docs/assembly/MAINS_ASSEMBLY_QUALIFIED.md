# Mains Assembly — Qualified Personnel Only

**Status:** `BLOCKED` — no approved mains design, no lab, no sign-off.

## Preconditions

- Engineer-approved rating chain, isolation, PE, fuse/relay/terminal/socket, enclosure.
- Lab with isolation transformer/RCD, rated probes, fire safety, two-person rule.
- Written procedure and stop conditions.
- No 3D-printed enclosure for mains.

## Work items

1. PE bond: dedicated stud/terminal, lock washer, torque, continuity test point.
2. Fuse/MOV/TCO/relay/PSU mounted with clearance and thermal spacing.
3. Terminal blocks with ferrules, correct torque, strain relief.
4. Socket retained mechanically; PE contact verified.
5. Cable gland + internal anchor; no load on solder/header.
6. Barrier intact; no mains wire crosses SELV zone.
7. Label rating/warnings.

## Inspection before energy

- [ ] PE continuity plug→socket (no relay/fuse).
- [ ] L/N isolation, no short.
- [ ] Creepage/clearance worksheet signed.
- [ ] Fuse/relay/terminal/socket ratings verified.
- [ ] Thermal cutoff clamped to hotspot.
- [ ] Enclosure rated; no printed part in mains path.
- [ ] Lab procedure and emergency plan.

## Do not

- Do not energize without engineer/lab.
- Do not perform hipot/short-circuit tests without rated equipment/operator.
- Do not bypass PE/fuse/relay/lockout.
